The workflow produces JSON sampled mesh patches and a self-contained HTML review page with XY, XZ, and YZ projections. Select left/right, relationship, and donor/host visibility. Each model remains in its original coordinate frame; there is no cross-model registration.

Endpoint crops are AABB-derived geometric regions, not anatomical articular segmentation. They do not model cartilage, menisci, ligaments, joint capsule, or contact mechanics and cannot establish clinical joint space or congruence.

Read-only diagnostic output. No production viewer, geometry, transforms, manifests, or deployment are modified.
